# Chromatic Dream Logic

**System:** Moon Image Cortex · **Type:** conditional visual specialist · **Status:** active
**Parent authority:** [Visual Director](../../VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md#first-use) · [Component index](../../docs/COMPONENT_INDEX.md)
**Renderer:** external and optional; this file contains no image generator.

## Component activation

Load this contract **only** when the Visual Director selects Chromatic Dream Logic for an actual visual task. If using this file in isolation, first state the task, source permissions, intended output, and tool availability; do not infer that a renderer or identity profile is installed. The Visual Director resolves conflicts and controls handoff. The specialist supplies its own material, composition and QA rules.

## Specialist method

**Trigger:** The user wants a coherent visual language organized by color, material, sharpness and spatial logic without specifying a fixed subject theme.

**Visual DNA:** Treat it as a thematic-neutral meta-style compiler with one visual thesis. Coordinate a controlled palette of two to four colors, light, surface, edge treatment, depth and selective focus. It is a historical prompt grammar studied in the Midjourney V3 era, not an image model, API, weights, or claim of equivalent behavior.

**Composition and material:** Reserve crispness for the focal element; support it with atmospheric softness, tactile contrast, and deliberate negative space. Themes and motifs are selected from the brief.

**Must preserve:** User’s chosen subject and emotional intent; color structure should not replace meaning.

**Avoid:** Automatic moons, gothic symbols, cosmic space, ornate architecture, monochrome, or any predetermined theme.

**Direction example:** “A red ceramic seedling tray on a bright workbench; restrained coral, leaf green and warm ivory; crisp glazed rim, soft greenhouse background, angled sun, generous work surface, no mystical symbols.”

**Input → output:** Subject, mood, palette constraints and material cues → one visual thesis and theme-neutral direction.

**QA gate:** Check that palette and surface support the subject, focal hierarchy reads, and no inherited motif has displaced the brief.

**Synthetic case:** “Make a non-gothic chromatic image about a ceramic mug after rain.” Keep the theme ordinary and let water, glaze, window light and color carry the visual logic.

## Chromatic and compositional compiler

The engine recreates an aesthetic *logic*, not the inaccessible behavior, weights or copyrighted output of any commercial generator. Its working kernel is **semantic freedom + compositional certainty + chromatic orchestration + tactile mediation + emotional atmosphere**.

### Ten aesthetic invariants

1. **One clear visual thesis.** A coherent image from a distance, intriguing detail up close.
2. **Assertive structure.** Large masses and unmistakable focal hierarchy; diagonals, depth, asymmetry or natural framing where justified.
3. **Orchestrated color.** Prefer two to four intentional color families, reserving saturated accents for light, focus or emotional tension.
4. **Light as material.** Dappled sun, colored shadow, gentle halation, prismatic reflection or volumetric sky when motivated by the world.
5. **Selective focus.** Keep narratively significant features clear; allow surroundings to soften instead of applying forensic sharpness everywhere.
6. **Tactile mediation.** Choose gouache, soft digital painting, old airbrush, print, color film, illustrated book or another *specific* medium.
7. **Semantic interpretation.** Translate concepts through posture, environment, scale, rhythm and weather instead of literal explanatory icons.
8. **Suggestive detail.** Density and texture support the whole rather than forming an inventory of random objects.
9. **Spatial emotion.** Place tenderness, solitude, awe or anticipation into distance, framing and light.
10. **Living idealization.** Beauty retains vulnerability, material imperfection, gesture and believable embodied presence.

### Distinct operating modes

| Mode | Visual behavior |
|---|---|
| **Prism** | More intense chromatic contrast and refracted light, without full-spectrum chaos |
| **Illustrated World** | Pictorial environmental richness and clear world scale |
| **Soft Sublime** | Quiet monumental atmosphere, large negative space and restrained accents |
| **Luminous Intimacy** | Warm local light, close human/environmental relationships and tactile softness |
| **Monumental Organic** | Dominant landscape, architecture or natural form with a human-scale reference |

These are **steering modes**, not claims of reproducible external model presets. Choose only one as a primary operating regime, and vary it according to subject.

### Compilation order

Identify intent → define scene and dominant composition → fix large color-family relationships → establish light behavior → choose material finish → place meaningful detail → assign focus falloff → write negative guidance and inspect originality.

**Unifying rule:** one world, one light logic, one material family. Multiple distinct textures are possible, but they must belong to a common authored visual reality.

### Prompt example

“An original illustrated greenhouse at the moment of sunrise. A single gardener at medium distance in a large space defined by three strong masses: glass roof, dense leaves and narrow central path. Organize warm amber light against cool blue-green shade, with selective prismatic glints in wet glass, painterly gouache texture and soft peripheral detail. Keep gesture legible. Do not borrow a reference greenhouse's camera position or object arrangement.”

### Anti-drift and review

Reject accidental duplication of reference scenes, obligatory cosmic imagery, color noise, universal bloom, fully detailed backgrounds, plastic skin and invented precise text. Pair with [Reference Abstraction Guardrail](../../components/references-and-identity/REFERENCE_ABSTRACTION_GUARDRAIL.md) whenever a reference corpus is used for aesthetic calibration.
