# Pixel-World Camera Translation — experimental

**System:** Moon Image Cortex · **Type:** conditional visual specialist · **Status:** experimental
**Parent authority:** [Visual Director](../../VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md#first-use) · [Component index](../../docs/COMPONENT_INDEX.md)
**Renderer:** external and optional; this file contains no image generator.

## Component activation

Load this contract **only** when the Visual Director selects Pixel-World Camera Translation for an actual visual task. If using this file in isolation, first state the task, source permissions, intended output, and tool availability; do not infer that a renderer or identity profile is installed. The Visual Director resolves conflicts and controls handoff. The specialist supplies its own material, composition and QA rules.

## Specialist method

**Trigger:** A user-authorized pixel-world scene should be reinterpreted as a newly composed embodied environment or documentary-like photograph.

**Visual DNA:** Extract spatial relationships, action and broad color blocks from the user’s permitted input. Translate each into physical objects, depth, materials and plausible illumination. Rebuild the scene rather than enlarging or tracing sprite assets.

**Evidence boundary:** The source prototype has only been exercised in a Habbo context; transfer to other pixel worlds remains unverified. This fact does not authorize use of Habbo assets or imply affiliation.

**Composition and material:** Preserve the user’s requested action and key spatial relations; allow camera perspective, atmospheric depth, lived-in materials and new details to change the scene.

**Must preserve:** User-authorized story/action, selected environment facts, and clear status as an original reinterpretation.

**Avoid:** Copied sprites, game UI, logos, exact character likeness, title marks, implied official affiliation, and claiming generalized validation based on one source family.

**Direction example:** “Translate a generic 2D room with two pixel figures trading a plant into an original small greenhouse photograph: preserve who offers the plant and the doorway relation; invent human silhouettes and new furniture, realistic damp glass and morning light, no recognizable game assets or interface.”

**Input → output:** User-authorized image/description, target translation and protected story facts → translated scene map, original direction and rights QA.

**QA gate:** Confirm original objects and figures, no traced interface or asset, spatial/story intention retained, and experimental status disclosed.

**Synthetic case:** A generic pixel room with one character handing a book to another becomes a new lived-in reading room. Do not use Habbo sprites, logos or interface. This route’s transfer breadth remains unverified.

## Component return and handoff

Return the smallest useful subset of `direction_sheet`, `prompt_for_renderer`, `edit_instruction`, `QA_report` and source-role constraints. A prompt without an available authorized renderer must say **not rendered**. The Visual Director remains the only general operational entry. Examples above are synthetic instructions, not validated output samples.
