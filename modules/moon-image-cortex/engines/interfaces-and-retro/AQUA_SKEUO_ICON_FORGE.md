## Specialist method

**Trigger:** An original desktop icon, early mobile tile, skeuomorphic control, or tactile interface illustration is needed.

**Visual DNA:** Identify the object type first: desktop file/folder/app icon, mobile app tile, toolbar control, or interface component. Translate function into a simple physical metaphor, then decide material, bevel, shadow, gloss, perspective and background.

**Composition and material:** Prioritize one silhouette and one focal metaphor. Give gloss and relief a consistent light direction. Separate desktop-object perspective from a flat rounded-square mobile tile. A hybrid must be requested or explained.

**Must preserve:** Recognizability at small size, stated function, platform intent and contrast.

**Avoid:** Copied OS icons, trademarked symbols, default glass gradients, excessive reflections, multiple unrelated metaphors, unreadable microtext, and implying official platform affiliation.

**Direction example:** “Original early-mobile tile for a fictional voice memo app: rounded square, charcoal-blue enamel surface, one simple silver waveform inside a small physical cassette-window metaphor, upper-left soft highlight, crisp silhouette at 64 px, no logo or platform mark.”

**Input → output:** Function, platform/type, era, size, metaphor and palette → icon construction sheet, prompt, and small-size/legal QA.

**QA gate:** View at intended dimensions, test silhouette/function, inspect material lighting and confirm the mark is original.

**Synthetic case:** An imaginary note-taking app requests an Aqua-era icon. Create a paperclip-and-card metaphor; do not reproduce Apple’s compass, Finder, or another vendor icon.

## Visual systems handbook

### Choose the regime before drafting an icon

Aqua and skeuomorphism overlap historically, but **Mac OS X object icons, iOS 6 home-screen tiles and entire interface surfaces follow different geometries**. Choosing “glossy” is not a sufficient specification.

| Regime | Primary geometry | Required material decisions | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mac OS X Aqua object icon** | Independent physical object silhouette, usually on transparency, slight elevated desk-like perspective | Mass, front/top visible planes, one key light, deep coherent shadow and tactile surface | Automatic rounded-square phone-app tile |
| **iOS 6 home-screen icon** | Rounded tile with an internally legible physical metaphor | Base material, shallow bevel, inner depth, upper gloss, edge contrast and restrained external shadow | Flat monochrome glyph on a gradient |
| **Apple legacy hybrid** | New service translated into period object/tile logic | Invent a physical function metaphor rather than paste a modern logo | False claim of an official vintage icon |
| **Aqua/skeuomorphic interface** | Full responsive page or panel ecosystem | Cards, tabs, controls, hierarchy, mobile constraints and meaningful materials | Modern translucent glassmorphism substituted for period UI |

### Four governing laws

1. **Regime integrity:** the destination platform determines object/tile geometry. Do not mix a Dock object with a home-screen tile accidentally.
2. **Function before ornament:** identify the real action or service. A code repository might become a leather codebook, metal branching mechanism or small terminal plaque; messaging might use a radio, letter or physical notice board.
3. **Materiality is causal:** highlights follow curvature; glass refracts, leather absorbs, paper creases, metal reflects and felt diffuses. Gloss without a surface is empty decoration.
4. **Scale readability:** an icon must survive actual reduced-size inspection. The physical metaphor and external silhouette should remain recognizable without small letters.

### Mac OS X object build

Choose a unique physical silhouette, slightly raised front-top perspective and controlled drop shadow. Typical materials include translucent plastic, brushed metal, polished wood, optical glass, notebook paper, leather, enamel and small lens assemblies. Reserve saturated aqua reflections for appropriate materials, not every surface. A transparent output must truly omit all backdrop or preview tile.

### iOS 6 tile construction

Build layers in this order: substrate or textured base; functional object/mini-scene; inner shadow; controlled gloss on upper area; bevel and border separation; cast shadow if needed. Keep rounded-square proportions coherent. Useful material metaphors include stitched leather, ruled paper, felt, camera optics, calendar pages, map paper, gel buttons and warm polished wood. Avoid iOS 7 flatness and modern app-store minimalism.

### Interface-level constraints

For complete websites or educational visual atlases, a desktop composition must not be forced into a phone. Begin with a single-column mobile structure, test text on blue/glossy surfaces for contrast, control overflow and distinguish tactile panels from arbitrary translucent white haze. Buttons and language toggles must remain recognizable, and decorative shine must not prevent reading.

### Reusable direction examples

**Object icon:** “An original Aqua-era app icon for a field notebook service, shaped as a tactile pocket notebook with a polished metal hinge, subtle glass magnifier, one top-left light and a clean transparent background. Readable in a Dock, not enclosed in a phone tile.”

**Home-screen icon:** “An original 2011-era rounded-square tile for a household recipe app; stitched leather base, one tactile recipe card, soft upper gloss, shallow bevel and balanced shadow. Legible at launcher size; no copied app icon or logo.”

**Modern function, period metaphor:** “A repository collaboration platform expressed as an original branching mechanical tool and bound code journal, not its contemporary brand mark.”

### Six-part QA

- **Regime:** Is the icon clearly object, tile, hybrid or interface?
- **Materials:** Does reflectance and contact shadow make physical sense?
- **Function:** Is meaning visible without microcopy?
- **Era:** Is the result plausible in the chosen visual period rather than present-day glassmorphism?
- **Legal/brand:** Does it avoid official icon replication, logo substitution and implied affiliation?
- **Artifacts:** Any melted motifs, tiled microtexture, unreadable tiny details, inconsistent shadows or unwanted canvas?

Deliver a direction sheet, render prompt or a *specific* QA correction. The file itself contains no image editor.
