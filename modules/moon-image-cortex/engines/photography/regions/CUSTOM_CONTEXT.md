# Custom Geographic Camera Context · Any Region

**Status:** available for any location; **country is optional**.
**Parent:** [Contextual Nostalgic Camera](../CONTEXTUAL_NOSTALGIC_CAMERA.md) · [Regional registry](README.md) · [Visual Director](../../../VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md#first-use)

## Open geography schema

Use `country` (or multiple countries) only if the task needs it. `region`, `city`, `neighborhood`, `time_period`, `scene_type`, `capture_device`, `print_or_scan_history`, `community_context`, `reference_roles`, `verified_details`, `unknowns`, `permissions`, `excluded_stereotypes` and `sources` remain user-defined and optional.

**Country is not ethnicity, ancestry or passport status.** When no location is provided, leave all geographic fields unspecified and write a plausible but unlocalized scene. If the user chooses another country, subregion, historical border, diaspora context or multilingual household, create a bounded lens from those facts; do not pretend a missing preset is an error.

## Regional-context compilation

- Ground period and place in user instructions or appropriately dated visual/source records.
- Preserve what is visually observed separately from what a caption or human says.
- Choose the scene's physical facts first; localized props must be evidence-backed or user-requested.
- Photograph plausibility comes from device, optics, light, medium, processing and handling, not a national filter.
- If dates are politically/historically sensitive, do not infer the contemporary country name was in use then.
- Do not use reference imagery without permission; an archival catalog is a research lead, not a rights grant.

## Synthetic example

“A late-1980s family album image at an unspecified seaside town.” Keep town and nationality unknown until supplied; compose from beach light, the camera and the event, not inferred flags or clothing.

## Output

Local context sheet with confidence and sources, optional visual instructions and a stereotype/rights gate. Without a renderer, **not rendered**.
