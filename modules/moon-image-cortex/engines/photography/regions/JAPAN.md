# Japan · Geographic Camera Context

**Status:** optional region-specific context adapter, **not** a default nationality preset.
**Parent:** [Contextual Nostalgic Camera](../CONTEXTUAL_NOSTALGIC_CAMERA.md) · [Regional registry](README.md) · [Visual Director](../../../VISUAL_DIRECTOR.md#first-use)

This is a source-finding and historical-context lens, not a template for people's bodies, race, ethnicity, personality, consumer wealth, architecture, or clothing. It activates only from the user's choice or credible scene evidence, not from a subject's appearance, inferred language or the user's location. The user may override or combine its suggestions.

## Context rules

**Japan is historically and geographically diverse.** Specify region, decade and social setting; urban Tokyo, Okinawa and rural communities are not interchangeable. Avoid automatic cherry blossoms, tatami, shrines, anime styling or uniform-camera claims.

Before adding any local object or photographic convention, establish the requested decade, city/region when relevant, event, camera type and nature of available evidence. The same country can include multiple communities, media, access patterns, climate zones and print histories. Default to ordinary unmarked surroundings where details are unknown. Do not change a person's face or self-described identity to match the region.

## Research lead and evidence ceiling

[Research archive or collection](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/en/rnavi/humanities/link_photography). Japan's National Diet Library provides photographic research routes; historic collections may cover periods other than the requested one.

The reference identifies where historical research could begin. It does **not** verify a particular decade's home furnishings or camera for every community, and images may have separate reuse restrictions. Consult a dated source relevant to the user's actual place and time before asserting specific objects. Do not copy archive imagery into prompts or packages without permission.

## Handoff

Supply scene context, source references, locale confidence, period uncertainty and a short anachronism/anti-stereotype checklist to the parent photographic engine. Any renderer remains external; **not rendered** is required absent real output.
