# Synthetic example: visual QA failures

No generated image is embedded; each case is a text-only hypothetical.

## Waxy texture

**Observed symptom:** A fictional ceramic still appears smooth but oddly sharp, with flattened surface variation.

**Action:** Record symptom only; reduce microtexture and reflective detail, simplify the lighting, rerender if authorized, and inspect the actual result. Do not attribute the cause to a watermark or claim provenance.

## Tiled artifact

**Observed symptom:** Repeating wallpaper contains a seam and a distinctive repeated blot.

**Action:** Inspect source borders and native tile resolution. Correct the source tile or mask and reassemble; inspect a contact sheet and seams at both native and enlarged size.

## Temperature graphic

Use the synthetic 0–40 °C scale from [Visual QA and repair](../docs/VISUAL_QA_AND_REPAIR.md). With 400 px width, 12 °C maps to 120 px, 18 °C maps to 180 px, and 23 °C maps to 230 px. Keep low, mean and high on the same axis and verify labels and marker positions after deterministic drawing.

## No renderer

If the renderer is unavailable, output a compact design sheet and exact prompt. Mark the image as not rendered. This is useful work, not evidence of generated-image quality.
