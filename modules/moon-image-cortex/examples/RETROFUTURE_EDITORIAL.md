# Synthetic example: retrofuture editorial

**Input:** “Make a 1970s-inspired poster about a future public rooftop garden connected to transit.”

**Route:** Retrofuturo Habitável.

**Visual thesis:** In a city designed around shared mobility, the roof becomes a common garden and waiting place rather than a private luxury.

**Direction:** Original civic building cutaway, visible transit connection, people using shaded garden terraces, restrained warm ink palette, offset-print texture, strong editorial grid, invented agency name and headline. Show accessibility and public use. Do not copy a real company campaign or source composition.

**QA:** Historical perspective is present in the imagined future; infrastructure and lived experience connect; type reads at poster distance; no copied logo, slogan, layout or character.

**Lineage:** The method records a counterfactual-advertising influence in [Originality, attribution and claims](../docs/ORIGINALITY_ATTRIBUTION_AND_CLAIMS.md). This fictional example reproduces no source asset or prompt.
