# Synthetic Example: Multitrack Case with Legal Boundary

This case is fictional and jurisdiction-neutral. It demonstrates parallel social navigation and a narrow legal handoff; it is not legal advice or a real case study.

## The situation

In the fictional city of Lumen, Sam receives a written notice from a housing provider stating that the household must leave within a short period. Sam also depends on the unit’s accessibility features and has no confirmed alternative housing. The notice’s legal effect, review period and available protections are unknown.

At the same time, Sam needs immediate help preserving shelter, medication access and transportation. The social and legal questions are related but not identical.

## Current state and desired passage

```text
current state: written housing notice; accessible home at risk; essential support tied to location
desired state: immediate safety and shelter continuity while the notice is reviewed
social tracks: emergency housing prevention, medication and accessible transport
legal track: determine what the notice means, what deadline applies and who can review it
```

## Evidence and claim ceiling

- **Record:** a written notice exists and should be preserved with its date and delivery context.
- **Reported:** Sam says the unit’s accessibility features are necessary for daily functioning.
- **Unknown:** whether the notice is valid, enforceable, reviewable or subject to a specific protection.
- **Not established:** illegality, discrimination, bad faith or liability.

The navigator should not turn the notice into a legal conclusion merely because it looks urgent or unfair.

## Parallel tracks

### Social track: protect the material floor

Identify the current housing-prevention or emergency-support intake for Sam’s service area, ask what immediate protection or assessment exists and preserve medication and transport continuity. The first social door should be able to triage or redirect the shelter risk.

**Owner:** the identified service or protection authority for intake; Sam or a chosen supporter for the bounded contact.  
**Checkpoint:** intake receipt, emergency assessment, confirmed alternative or documented failure.

### Legal-boundary track: clarify the notice

Prepare a narrow packet for a qualified legal owner:

```text
question: what does the notice require, by when, and what review or protection route may apply?
jurisdiction: Lumen housing system, to be verified
chronology: notice received → current response window → contacts attempted
record: notice preserved in original form
impact: accessible housing and essential support may be disrupted
unknowns: service, deadline, current status and legal effect
request: legal review of effect, deadline, options and evidence preservation
```

**Owner:** qualified lawyer, public defender or appropriate legal service.  
**Checkpoint:** intake accepted, deadline clarified, document request or legal route identified.

## Safety override

If the notice is accompanied by immediate loss of shelter, violence or inability to obtain essential medication, the protective or emergency route takes priority. The legal packet can remain bounded and parallel; it must not delay immediate safety.

## What this example demonstrates

Opening a legal track does not turn the navigation module into a law office. The module preserves the record, separates fact from conclusion, keeps material-support tracks moving and gives the legal owner a concrete question instead of an accusation or an undigested life story.
