# Synthetic Example: Service Access and Bureaucratic Block

This case is fictional and jurisdiction-neutral. It is a demonstration of institutional navigation, not a real resource recommendation.

## The situation

The fictional Vale household is struggling to keep food and utilities stable after a sudden income reduction. A neighbor mentioned a “household support program,” but the household does not know which authority owns it. A search result lists an old phone number; a current official page appears to direct residents to a different intake channel.

## Reconstructed problem

```text
surface request: find a benefit
functional problem: protect food and essential utilities during an income gap
jurisdiction gap: country, region and local service area are not yet confirmed
first useful door: current official intake or local support triage able to assess both needs
```

The navigator should not declare that the household qualifies for a benefit. It should first ask for the location information that changes the route, then verify the official owner and intake channel.

## Bounded research plan

1. Confirm the household’s country, region and local service area.
2. Search the responsible authority’s current official source.
3. Verify whether the route handles food, utilities or only one of them.
4. Confirm channel, hours, access conditions and documents to have ready.
5. Use the old phone number only as a recorded lead, not as current confirmation.
6. Ask for a protocol, intake receipt or specific redirect.

## Resource Pack shape

```text
need being solved: food and essential utilities during temporary income loss
jurisdiction: to be confirmed before recommendation
first useful door: current official local intake / triage channel
what it may do: screen needs, open intake or redirect to the responsible service
documents to have ready: only those confirmed by the current source
checked_on: fill at the time of live research
source: current official authority page or verified institutional channel
caveat: initial screening is not an eligibility decision
next-step owner: named household contact for intake; institution for assessment
checkpoint: protocol, redirect, decision or documented channel failure
```

## Institutional request

> We are trying to stabilize food and essential utility access after a temporary income reduction in **[service area]**. Which current service or intake channel can assess these needs? Please confirm the responsible unit, the documents to have ready and how to obtain a protocol. If this is not the correct door, please provide the specific redirect.

## Ownership and dead-end test

- The household owns the bounded act of confirming location and making the intake request.
- The institution owns screening, eligibility decision and redirect.
- A supervisor or ombuds becomes relevant only if the first channel fails and the escalation is proportionate.
- “Try another department” without a named route is an unresolved dead end.

## What this example demonstrates

The first useful door is not the most impressive program name. It is the current route capable of receiving the functional problem and creating the next owner. The old directory can be preserved as search history; it cannot govern the present.
