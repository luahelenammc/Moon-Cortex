# Core Invariants

These are the stable laws of the Social Support Navigation System. They are meant to survive localization and adaptation without turning the module into a professional authority or a fixed program directory.

## The invariants

1. **The person remains sovereign.** Support should increase the person’s ability to understand, choose and continue, whenever safety permits.
2. **Function comes before label.** Describe what is failing, being carried or at risk before relying on a diagnosis, category or program name.
3. **Transition comes before resource lists.** A resource matters only in relation to the passage it may help create.
4. **Replace functions, not people.** The goal is to transfer unsustainable functions from informal support to appropriate capacity, not to erase relationships by default.
5. **Informal support has finite material capacity.** Time, money, health, work, sleep, housing and emotional load are part of the case state.
6. **Choose a first useful door.** Prefer a route that can triage, assume, coordinate or redirect the need over an unranked pile of links.
7. **Every meaningful next step has an owner.** “They,” “the family” or “someone” is not an operational owner.
8. **Review on events.** A protocol, response, referral, denial, assessment, safety change or removed barrier is a better checkpoint than elapsed time alone.
9. **Triage is not eligibility.** Screening can identify what is worth investigating; only the competent authority decides eligibility, priority, service or benefit.
10. **Use official current sources for unstable resources.** Programs, contacts, hours, requirements, availability and channels need jurisdiction and freshness.
11. **Evidence comes before accusation.** Separate fact, record, report, indication, inference, hypothesis and legal qualification.
12. **Safety can override gradualism.** A graceful transition is not a reason to keep someone in immediate danger or without essential support.
13. **Privacy follows minimum necessity.** Access to a corpus does not authorize processing, disclosure, publication or ownership of everything in it.
14. **Social and legal tracks can coexist.** A legal question may require a qualified owner while material support and navigation continue in parallel.
15. **Local state remains locally sovereign.** The public module does not become the only place where a person’s case can be recovered or continued.
16. **Loops must close.** A track ends as resolved, externally owned and pending, blocked, escalated, intentionally paused or closed.
17. **Authority follows the domain.** Clinical, legal, emergency, public-authority, regulated-service and eligibility decisions belong to their competent owners.
18. **Form must not overclaim.** A polished document does not create a license, institutional mandate, validated score or guaranteed outcome.

## Operational consequences

These invariants imply a compact cycle:

```text
real situation → function → barrier → first useful door
→ owner → event checkpoint → adjusted or closed track
```

If a case is simple, use only the part of the cycle that changes the next action. If a case is complex, preserve a resumable local state without turning every detail into active work.

## Public boundary

The invariants are generalized architecture, not a substitute for local law, professional standards, safeguarding procedures, accessibility practice or institutional policy. They do not authorize a user or an AI to decide matters reserved for a competent authority.
