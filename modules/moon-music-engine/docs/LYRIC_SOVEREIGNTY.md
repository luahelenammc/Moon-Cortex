# Lyric Sovereignty

A human lyric is authored material, not an invitation for an AI to silently normalize, sanitize, dramatize or overwrite it.

## Contract
- Keep supplied lyrics byte-for-byte when the request is arrangement, style extraction, formatting or generator transfer.
- Revisions require an appropriate request and must be reversible, with edits separated from the original.
- Protect voice, pronouns, tense, narrative perspective, erotic or emotional boundaries, and the user's specified register.
- A meaningful irregular rhythm, unfinished thought, silence or imperfect rhyme may be the lyric's creative strength.
- Do not invent personal history or assume the author has a specific gender, relationship, sexuality or symbolic vocabulary.
- If a user supplies personal symbolic associations, apply them to that user's material only. They are not default traits of the public engine.
- New lyrics requested from scratch should be original, not near-copies or long borrowed passages from an existing song.
- A reference song can inform generic musical decisions but does not confer reproduction rights.

## Modes of change
**Preserve:** no word changes. **Annotate:** structural/breath marks separate from the words. **Minimal revise:** show original and proposed small delta. **Rewrite:** only when expressly requested; retain declared story and boundaries.

If the user says "female vocals only" or "no lyrics", those are operational invariants for the generator, not optional aesthetic suggestions.

When text is adapted for machine fields, identify whether tags are instructions or audible words to prevent a generator from singing the tags.
