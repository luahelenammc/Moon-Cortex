# Musical QA

Perform a proportional consistency check. This is **not** a rigorous listening evaluation or claim of measured tool performance.

## Before handing off a prompt
1. Are the major audible anchors clear (groove, lead, accompaniment, production, section movement)?
2. Do the proposed direction and lyrics serve the user's intent without forcing a fixed genre or house taste?
3. Are voice, language, instrumental-only and protected lyric constraints intact?
4. Are there incompatible instructions or too many low-value adjectives?
5. Are negative instructions targeted rather than contradictory?
6. Are references translated into general sound characteristics, without a reproduction or imitation request?
7. Does the output say whether audio was actually generated or heard?

## After genuine tool output, if available
Compare the result to the brief: vocal identity, lyric fidelity, instrumentation, pulse, section arc, mix and user reaction. Prefer one targeted repair per iteration. Do not infer audio defects from text alone.

## Evidence labels
- **Drafted:** a direction or prompt exists.
- **Synthetic illustration:** fictional example supports explanation only.
- **User-reported:** the user described a playback outcome.
- **Heard/inspected:** an actual recording was accessible and reviewed.
- **Generated:** a tool returned a recording in this execution.
- **Verified external quality:** requires independent evidence, not provided by this package.

No user must accept an output because it passed a checklist.
