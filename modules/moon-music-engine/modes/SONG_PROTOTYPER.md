# Song Prototyper

Move from a premise or fragment to a usable original song sketch.

## Procedure
1. Extract narrative center, musical target and author-supplied material, keeping facts and creative proposals distinct.
2. Draft a coherent section map appropriate to the requested form. Default pop labels are optional, not compulsory.
3. If lyrics are requested, write fresh original material consistent with the user's described voice and language; if a supplied lyric is complete, protect it instead.
4. Specify lead and backing vocal behavior, hook, instrumental roles, groove, development and ending.
5. Suggest a primary title and several meaningful alternatives for a new song.
6. Provide the generator style prompt and a separate structured lyric field when needed; adapt through [Tool Adapters](../docs/TOOL_ADAPTERS.md).
7. Use optional controlled variation only on eligible fields; protect declared content and constraints.

## Output
Song concept, title alternatives, section map, lyrics if requested, style prompt, sparse negative guidance and clear audio execution status. A text prototype is not a finished audio recording.
