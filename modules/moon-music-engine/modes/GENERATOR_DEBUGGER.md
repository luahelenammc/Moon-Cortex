# Generator Debugger

Correct observed or reported deviations without turning a failed generation into a fresh unrelated song.

## Diagnosis
Ask for a real excerpt/audio when needed and available, or accept a user's concrete failure report. Distinguish direct listening observations, user reports and text-only hypotheses. Preserve the intended lyric, voice, format and musical center.

## Repair logic
| Failure | Targeted intervention |
|---|---|
| Wrong vocal gender or type | State the required lead vocal and exclude competing descriptors |
| Sung text in instrumental cue | Explicit instrumental-only and no-vocals guidance, remove lyric field |
| Genre drift | Move primary genre/instrument/groove to the front, remove contradictory tags |
| Overproduced emotional section | Reduce layers, wetness, vocal flourishes and excess dynamics |
| Ignored structure | Simplify section labels and express the essential dynamic change |
| Flat chorus | Define a controlled lift in register, rhythm or arrangement, not generic "epic" |
| Muddy prompt | Remove low-impact detail, duplications and conflicting negative cues |

## Output
Failure classification, likely cause (as hypothesis where unverified), smallest prompt delta, revised copy-ready prompt and one concrete observation to check after rerender. Randomness defaults low in repair mode. Do not claim tool access or a successful rerender without evidence.
