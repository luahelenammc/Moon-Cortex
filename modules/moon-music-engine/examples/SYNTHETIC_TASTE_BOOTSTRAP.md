# Synthetic Taste Profile · Worked Example

**Completely fictional scenario.** Track titles, observations and tastes below were invented for this tutorial; they are not extracted from any real person's listening profile or platform dataset.

## Listener's own description

"I love *Paper Lanterns* because its gentle rhythm supports a slowly unfolding melody. I play *Harbor Machines* when focusing on work, especially for the woody plucked sounds. *Chrome Parade* has a fun pulse but tires me when its sharp noise continues too long. *Summer Atlas* used to be a favorite, but lately I'm overplaying it."

The named tracks are imaginary; no audio has been played or verified.

## Taste Cartographer maps mechanisms

| Signal | Interpretation | Evidential limit |
|---|---|---|
| Paper Lanterns · love | patient melodic rise plus gently active rhythm | user-reported, one example |
| Harbor Machines · context like | plucked texture for focus | not necessarily a universal instrument preference |
| Chrome Parade · mixed reaction | danceable energy can coexist with fatigue from continuous harsh treble | does not imply dislike of all electronic music |
| Summer Atlas · overplayed | historical affection plus current saturation | not an anti-taste judgment |

**C1 · Patient rhythmic emergence**, tentative Current Orbit; **C2 · Textured focus**, conditional on listening purpose; **A1 · Harshness fatigue**, contextual anti-taste; **S1 · Saturation**, without erasing previous positive evidence. One track is not enough to promote a Deep Core.

**Bridge hypothesis:** soft percussive propulsion plus a new plucked timbre, with gradual melodic development. The engine could search for real permitted candidates later, but must not fabricate music titles.

## Optional creation request

User: "Use that profile to compose an original instrumental for my evening; give it a little surprise."

**Selective bridge:** C1, a context-scoped part of C2, avoid sustained harsh treble, leave room for an unfamiliar counterline.

**Style prompt prepared, not rendered:**

> Intimate instrumental folktronica with gently syncopated hand percussion, gradual melodic emergence, softly plucked counterlines, rounded low-end and transparent midrange. Introduce one unusual organic rhythmic texture in the later section. No vocals and no persistent abrasive top-end.

A different instruction such as "make abrasive punk that challenges my taste" would override the profile. The user can opt out of using the profile entirely.

## Feedback and smallest patch

If the person listens to a real generated version and says "I loved the plucked melody, but hated the shaker in the ending," update the conditional percussion exception and its supporting evidence. **Do not** generalize this to "hates percussion" or replace the complete Taste State. Without storage authorization, return the proposed delta without claiming to have saved it.
