# Synthetic Creative Cases

These original, fictional examples illustrate method routing. They do not establish actual music generation or listening quality.

## 1 · Original human lyric, conservative direction
**User:** "Keep every word of my verse; make a subtle soul-pop arrangement."  
**Route:** Lyric Doctor (preserve-only) + Style Architect.  
**Output:** section labels separate from the original words; low-register vocal, warm bass, electric piano and subdued drums; no rewritten verse; prompt marked not rendered.

## 2 · One-sentence premise
**User:** "An instrumental scene where a train arrives at dawn."  
**Route:** Song Prototyper + Style Architect.  
**Output:** quiet metronomic percussion → widening strings/synth harmony → short arrival flourish → silence. No lyric field.

## 3 · Intense exploration, fixed words
**User:** "I wrote the lyric; try 90% sonic variation but keep it intact."  
**Route:** Controlled Variation.  
**Output:** an unexpected trip-hop/chamber-folk arrangement, protected lyric unchanged, suggested title variants optional. Percentage states exploration breadth, not acoustic probability.

## 4 · Failed output
**User:** "The track gave me a male lead instead of the female one I requested."  
**Route:** Generator Debugger.  
**Output:** lead identity moved to opening of style prompt, competing baritone/male choir removed, explicit negative lead rule and a single check for next render.

## 5 · Reference without copying
**User:** "I like the dynamic shape of a 1980s power ballad, but don't copy a song."  
**Route:** Style Architect.  
**Output:** gated drum atmosphere, slow-building electric guitars, lifted refrain, spacious reverb, no identifiable melody or lyrics from another work.

## 6 · Non-English lyric
**User:** "Keep these Portuguese verses; use an English style prompt."  
**Route:** Lyric Doctor preserve-only + Tool Adapter.  
**Output:** original Portuguese lyric plus English technical prompt and explicit "Portuguese lyrics", without translating lyrics or changing pronouns.
