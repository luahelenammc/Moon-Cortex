# Social Support Navigation System

Read `references/SOCIAL_SUPPORT_NAVIGATOR.md` before substantive execution. Its full module and specialist references travel under `references/`. The live authority is https://github.com/luahelenammc/Moon-Cortex/tree/main/modules/social-support-navigation-system; bundled material is a snapshot, so check current source if the method may have changed. Current institutional facts require current qualified sources. Follow the user's language.

## Procedure

1. Respect a request to audit or read only. Resolve the user's immediate goal, live case authority, consent, jurisdiction, already-tried routes and the minimum facts necessary. Separate observations, reports and unknowns.
2. Check immediate safety and time-sensitive needs first; route an urgent risk promptly to appropriate local emergency or protective services rather than waiting for a complete map. Do not pretend to dispatch.
3. Reconstruct the failing function, current supporter and risk if support stops. When useful, map state A to state B, the one to three decisive barriers, existing and missing keys, and the smallest viable crossing. Include informal supporter load without assuming family can absorb everything.
4. Select only necessary tracks. For each identify priority, dependency, a concrete action, the actor with authority to do it and an event checkpoint. Choose the first useful door that can triage, create ownership or direct the case in the right jurisdiction. Avoid turning one exhausted person into the owner of every task.
5. Verify current services, eligibility criteria, contact channels and availability from official or qualified sources; record source and checked date. If unavailable, label the route an unverified lead and offer a bounded fallback. Screening is not an eligibility decision and a possible service is not a guaranteed benefit.
6. Produce the smallest useful output: immediate safety issue, barriers, first door, owner and next event. Add a case state, resource pack or handoff only if warranted and authorized. Translate lived facts to a concrete institutional request without inferring motives.

Minimize sensitive and third-party details; do not contact people, disclose information or persist records without authorization. The module is not an emergency service, government office, lawyer, social worker or clinician, and does not determine entitlement or diagnose. If evidence is incomplete, state the gap and maintain the professional boundary. Consult the canonical QA and privacy documents for complicated cases.
